### Python List Manipulation Techniques

The instructor reviewed fundamental list manipulation methods, including:

- **Reverse Method vs. Slicing:** Instructor emphasized the critical differences between these approaches. The `reverse()` method modifies the original list in place, while slicing (`[::-1]`) creates a new reversed version without altering the original list.

- **Copy Method:** Introduction to creating new lists by copying elements from existing lists, with discussion on when and why copying is necessary.

- **List Assignment vs. Reference:** Instructor explained that assigning one list to another using the equal operator (`=`) creates a reference rather than a copy. This means changes to the original list will affect the assigned list. He demonstrated proper copying using the `copy()` method to avoid unintended modifications.

### Shallow vs. Deep Copying

The session included detailed coverage of copying mechanisms:

- **Shallow Copies:** Appropriate for simple, single-level lists
- **Deep Copies:** Required for nested lists to ensure complete independence from the original structure
